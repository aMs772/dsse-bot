## Slide 6 — Structure and Gates

The venture development pathway is **Level 0 → Level 1 → Level 2**. The slide marks key gates as **IDEA Triggers**, **Selection**, **Graduate**, and **Demo Day**.

### Level 0 — Innovation Triggers
Activities and support:
- Idea generation
- Team building
- Start-up Clinic / Mentor Hours
- ENT courses

### Level 1 — Problem–Solution Fit
- Duration: 3-month structured programme
- Grant: **₹1 lakh** (approximately **US$1,053**)
- Alumni support: **1 alumni mentor**
- Weekly coaching on problem–solution fit, customer persona and product concept
- Evidence-based decision to **go, pivot or no-go**

### Level 2 — Product–Market Fit
- Duration: 12-month structured programme
- Grant: **₹5 lakhs** (approximately **US$5,263**)
- Alumni support: **2 alumni mentors**
- Workshops on technology and NPD, legal matters, finance, leadership, sales and marketing
- Building an MVP and testing for product–market fit

### Programme figures highlighted on the slide
- **527** Level 1 teams
- **190** Level 2 teams
- **1,817** aspiring entrepreneurs
- **100+** mentors
- **123** registered startups
- **8** years

## Slide 7 — Operationalizing Level 1 Cohort

The Level 1 programme is a **3-month cohort-based pre-incubation programme** for early-stage venture development.

- **3-month cohort-based programme:** an intensive, structured journey for early-stage venture development.
- **Focus on problem–solution fit:** helps teams identify, validate and develop solutions for real-world problems.
- **Fortnightly masterclasses and workshops:** sessions on early-stage venture topics led by domain experts.
- **Fortnightly team webinars:** teams share progress, receive mentor input and get feedback.
- **Finale presentation at the end of the cohort:** teams may graduate to Level 2, continue in Level 1, or pivot.
- **Team grant of ₹1 lakh** (approximately US$1,053): early funding support to accelerate execution.

The slide repeats the approximate exchange rate of US$1 ≈ ₹95.

## Slide 8 — Operationalizing Level 2 Cohorts

The Level 2 programme is a **1-year self-paced venture development programme** designed to help teams build and scale their ventures.

- **1-year self-paced programme:** a flexible 12-month journey to build and scale a venture.
- **Focus on product–market fit:** validates product–market fit and supports sustainable businesses.
- **Prototype and MVP with business plan:** build MVPs and develop robust business plans with structured guidance.
- **Masterclasses on IP, finance and legal:** expert-led sessions covering key foundational areas.
- **Mentor support on production / GTM:** one-on-one mentoring to help teams build, launch and scale. GTM means go-to-market.
- **Investor presentations:** pitch to investors and ecosystem partners to accelerate growth.
- **Team grant of ₹5 lakhs** (approximately US$5,263): seed funding for product development and market entry.

### Level 2 programme figures shown
- **190** teams selected
- **422** participants
- **76** presented to SINE
- **49** accepted for IOE grants

The slide uses an approximate exchange rate of US$1 ≈ ₹95.

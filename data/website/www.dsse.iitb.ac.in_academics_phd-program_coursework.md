## COURSEWORK

We are committed to providing high quality training to doctoral scholars through a comprehensive coursework in the first year of the candidate’s program where (s)he will credit courses across three distinct buckets:

- Research Foundations – methodology and research philosophy courses
- Domain Introduction – fundamental concepts and theories in the domain of innovation and entrepreneurship
- Domain Specialization – advanced courses towards specific research interest of the scholar

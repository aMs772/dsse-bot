Business Fundamentals for Technopreneurs

Spring

3 credits

## Objective & Learning Outcomes

Businesses today are very complex and multi-disciplinary operations. Aspiring entrepreneurs and managers need to have a broad based overview of how companies operate and succeed. This course introduces the Fundamentals of Business to the learners.

## Course Modules

01. Business and Business Environment
02. Company Structure, Goals, Strategy & Values
03. New Product Development, Manufacturing & Quality Management
04. Leading Teams & Human Resource Management
05. Marketing. Branding & Advertising
06. B2B / B2C Sales & Distribution Channels
07. Customer Service & Advocacy.
08. Supply Chain & Logistics
09. Finance & Accounting
10. Environment Society & Governance

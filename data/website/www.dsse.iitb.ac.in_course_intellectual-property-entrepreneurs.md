ENT 601

Intellectual Property for Entrepreneurs

Spring

3 credits

## Objective & Learning Outcomes

Introduce students to various types of IP rights and how they can be utilized for protecting their creations and leverage them for future commercial benefits. The course involves individual assignments and team projects through which students will learn to apply the concepts learned. This involves patent searching and analysis for research and technology management as well as and IP-based case studies.

## Course Modules

**Modules**

1. Fundamental of Intellectual Property, Patents and Patenting Systems
2. Patent Information- Databases and Applications
3. Copyrights, Trade Marks and Designs
4. Other Forms of IP and Trade Secrets
5. Protection of Software and other contemporary topics
6. IP Asset Management

Introduction to Business Models

Autumn

6 credits

## Objective & Learning Outcomes

This course introduces students to the concept of business models and explain in detail the elements that make up a business model. The course studies business models across sectors, as well as digital business models, AI first business models and circular economy business models. Students learn how to develop and design a business model, using the Business Model Canvas framework, and apply their knowledge to a business idea. Lastly, we study how the digital and the Gen AI revolutions impact existing businesses and their business models.

## Course Modules

1. What are business models and why do they matter to a business
2. Value creation and value proposition in a startup
3. Designing a business model
4. Different types of business models and their elements
5. Impact of digitization and Gen AI on businesses and business models
6. Circular Economy Business Models
7. Digital and AI First Business Models

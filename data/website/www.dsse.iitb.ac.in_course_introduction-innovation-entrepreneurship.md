ENT 101

Introduction to Innovation & Entrepreneurship

Autumn & Spring (Core)

4 credits

## Objective & Learning Outcomes

Expose UG students in their first year to ‘entrepreneurial thinking & mindset’ by introducing various elements of venture creation including basic concepts in innovation and entrepreneurship. It will also help build generalist skills in UG students and aid them in becoming well-rounded individuals and preparing for careers beyond core engineering.

## Course Modules

1. Basics of Creativity, Innovation, Product Development
2. Market opportunity identification - Basics
3. Customer value proposition - Basics
4. Basics of finance and business planning
5. Marketing and Go To Market Planning

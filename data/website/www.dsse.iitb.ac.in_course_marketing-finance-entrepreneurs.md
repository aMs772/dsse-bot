ENT 609

Marketing & Finance for Entrepreneurs

Autumn

6 credits

## Objective & Learning Outcomes

This course is designed for aspiring Technology Entrepreneurs who are keen to convert their ideas & innovations into successful, sustainable and profitable startups. The curriculum provides a structured & holistics overview of various Customer Engagement, Marketing & Finance skills that are required for the Entrepreneurial Journey from \`Idea-to-IPO\`. Students will be required to work in teams on real-world customer & market discovery, customer value proposition & solution design, go-to-market plans, financial plans & investor pitch. Teams will be required to develop and present detailed startup business plans and investor pitch decks.

## Course Modules

01. Understanding why Startups Succeed or Fail
02. Self Discovery & Entrepreneurial Readiness Assessment
03. Startup Team Formation
04. Customer Problem Identification & Market Assessment
05. Customer Value Proposition & Solution Design
06. Customer Pricing
07. Branding & Advertisement
08. Supply Chain & Sales Channels
09. Customer Service & Advocacy
10. Sales Team and Tools
11. Accounting Concepts & Definition
12. Basics of Finance
13. Startup Financing
14. Startup Company Structure
15. Environment, Sustainability & Governance (ESG)
16. Investor Pitching

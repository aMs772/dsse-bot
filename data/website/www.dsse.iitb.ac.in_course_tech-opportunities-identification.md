## Objective & Learning Outcomes

DSSE runs foundational, specialist and capstone courses in Innovation and Entrepreneurship courses for aspiring entrepreneurs. These courses are typically differentiated from the management school courses, and bring in a different pedagogy - practice-oriented, guest lectures by entrepreneurs and experts, and experiential learning through project team work. DSSE currently doesn't have a course around Tech Opportunities and this learning could provide a head start to startup founders.

## Course Modules

Recognizing Entrepreneurial Opportunities and Generating Ideas

Types of Change (Technology, social and demographic factors, Economy and Industry factors)

Transforming science into Business

Technology, Industry and Competitor Analysis

Feasibility Analysis

Developing an Effective Business Model

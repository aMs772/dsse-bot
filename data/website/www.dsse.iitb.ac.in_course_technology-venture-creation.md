ENT 602

Technology Venture Creation

Autumn & Spring

6 credits

## Objective & Learning Outcomes

The purpose of this capstone course is to enable students to work on product development and business plan of tech entrepreneurial opportunities. Students are expected to participate in multiple do-it-yourself sessions to build product prototype. The course will help students develop a business plan around a tech idea by learning opportunity assessment, product development, go-to-market strategy and team building. It will help students get hands-on experience on the process of product development and start-up formation. Students are expected to develop the leadership skills required to begin their entrepreneurial journey.

## Course Modules

1. Purpose, Leadership & Team Building
2. Customer Discovery
3. Product Design, POC & Prototyping
4. Business Modelling
5. Startup Finance, Unit Economics
6. Product Development, Testing & Validation
7. Intellectual Property
8. Sales & Marketing
9. Startup Incorporation & Planning for success

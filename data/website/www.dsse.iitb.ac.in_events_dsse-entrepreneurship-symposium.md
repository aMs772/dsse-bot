DSSE Entrepreneurship Symposium

DSSE hosted a hybrid symposium on Entrepreneurship with in-person participants as well as online ones. The Chief Guest for the symposium was Prof Meric Gertler , President, University of Toronto. The symposium had key notes by the IITB Director, Prof Gertler, Prof Suresh Bhargava of RMIT, Melbourne and two panel discussions hosted by Prof Devdip Purkayastha and Prof Anuradha Narasimhan with panelists from academia and industry

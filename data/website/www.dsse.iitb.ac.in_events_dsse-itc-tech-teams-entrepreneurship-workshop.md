DSSE ITC Tech Teams - Entrepreneurship Workshop

DSSE faculty and team members organized a one-day workshop for the student teams of the Institute Technical Council – including the Racing Car Team, Team Shunya for Zero Energy, Aeromodelling Club, Autonomous Car Team etc. The objective of the workshop was to raise the aspiration levels for Entrepreneurship with these technically-oriented students and provide an overview on how technology could be converted into ventures

IIT Bombay Gen Zero Women Felicitation

In Sep 2023, Prof Anuradha Narasimhan was felicitated by IIT Bombay as an achiever-alumna in the initiative IIT Bombay Gen Zero Women. She received the award from two women achievers Ms. Falguni Nayar (Co-Founder Nykaa) and Ms Diana Eduljee (Captain - Indian Cricket Team) along with Prof Subhasis Chaudhuri, Director IIT Bombay

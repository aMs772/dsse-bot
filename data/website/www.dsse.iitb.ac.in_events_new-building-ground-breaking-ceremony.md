New Building Ground Breaking Ceremony

Ground breaking of the new DSSE building was held on 1st Dec 2022 and the building will take 24 months to be completed. The new building houses DSSE and Makerspace and is designed to educate, mentor and facilitate tech entrepreneurs. It houses several labs, a recording studio, class rooms, and hackathon and co-working spaces

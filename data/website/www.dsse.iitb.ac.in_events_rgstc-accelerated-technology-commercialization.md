RGSTC Accelerated Technology Commercialization

DSSE along with Rajiv Gandhi Science and Technology Commission of Govt of Maharashtra conducted a 3-day training workshop "Accelerating Technology Commercialization" to assist PI's in moving their research projects towards technology transfer / licensing / commercialization. 12 PI's participated in the workshop

## DSSE  \|  People

The team at the Desai Sethi School of Entrepreneurship (DSSE) comprises Institute faculty, Adjunct faculty, Post-doctoral fellows, Project Managers and Mentors. With the intent of creating a rounded academic unit focussing on academics, research and practice, the Institute faculty are drawn from academia and industry – the first Professors of Practice at IITB were recruited for DSSE in 2021. Starting 2023, DSSE recruited core faculty members whose research is in the areas of Innovation and Entrepreneurship.  DSSE has had adjunct faculty from entrepreneurship, mentoring, and industry backgrounds, who since inception have been instrumental in teaching practice-oriented and experiential courses.  With the start of its research program in 2024, DSSE also welcomes Post-Graduate fellows from eminent institutions and aligned research interests. DSSE’s project managers co-ordinate the academic and experiential programs and are always available to students for mentoring. The Post-Graduate Committee includes inter-disciplinary faculty members who guide and review various activities of the School. Mentors are drawn from the alumni community to coach and support course project teams as well as pre-incubation teams.

## Institute Faculty

DSSE got its full-time faculty from 2021 when the first two Professors of Practice were recruited by IIT Bombay for Desai Sethi School of Entrepreneurship and came with 25-30 years of industry experience. Starting 2023, DSSE has recruited core faculty members whose research will help build the areas of Innovation and Entrepreneurship – entrepreneurship education, lab to market translation, inclusive innovation and social entrepreneurship, entrepreneurship support ecosystem etc.

## Mentors

Several senior professionals from industry, many of them alumni of IIT Bombay, are associated with DSSE as mentors for aspiring entrepreneurs and venture teams pursuing their entrepreneurial ventures. These mentors serve two diverse roles – entrepreneur mentors provide their own experiences to young venture teams and subject matter experts provide mentoring in functional areas like technology, finance, business planning, Go to Market etc.

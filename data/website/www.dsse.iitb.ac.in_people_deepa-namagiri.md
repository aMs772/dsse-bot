## Biography

Deepa Namagiri is a Senior Project Manager at the Desai Sethi School of Entrepreneurship (DSSE), IIT Bombay, where she coordinates entrepreneurship courses. She holds an MBA in Marketing and Human Resources from Andhra University, India. Her previous roles include working at ICICI Bank in India, Clark Atlanta University in the USA, and the Texas Higher Education Board in the USA.

Since joining IIT Bombay in 2013, she has contributed to the ‘Train 10,000 Teachers (T10KT)’ and NVLI programs before transitioning to DSSE in 2021.

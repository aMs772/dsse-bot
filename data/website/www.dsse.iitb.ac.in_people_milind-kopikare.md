## Biography

Milind Kopikare is a technology executive who has helped build products from zero to a multi-million dollar business for many Silicon Valley companies including Qualtrics, Google, McKinsey and Marvell Semiconductors. He teaches Product Innovation and Entrepreneurship as an Adjunct Professor at the University of Utah Eccles School of Management and at IIT Bombay. His current research is in the area of using AI for products that are useful, usable and delightful.  He is also the President of Great Learning, an ed-tech company that is transforming skill and job based education for the global workforce.

Prior to this, he was the VP and Head of Product for Qualtrics and grew the Qualtrics SaaS business to a $400Mn ARR and an eventual acquisition by SAP for $8Bn.

He holds 21 patents in the areas of AI, Wireless Communication and AI Automation.

He is also an alum of Kellogg (Zell Scholar), Stanford (ISL Scholar), and IIT Bombay (Dr. S. D Sharma Gold Medalist).

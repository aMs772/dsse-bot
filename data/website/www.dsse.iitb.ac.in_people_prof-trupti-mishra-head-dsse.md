## Biography

Prof Trupti Mishra is a Professor at SJM School of Management with teaching interests in Managerial Economics, Circular Economy and Sustainable Finance. She is a founding member of IITB’s Climate Studies program as well as IITB’s Green Energy and Sustainability Hub (GESH) and her areas of research include Economic Assessment of Climate Change Vulnerability, Adaptation and Mitigation Options, Corporate Environmental Sustainability and Policy Studies. She has been instrumental in launching The Sustainability Action Lab (SAL) to bridge the gap between academic research and real-world sustainability challenges.  Prof Trupti Mishra has been a member of DSSE’s Post Graduate Committee and in that role has contributed significantly to the school’s PhD Program as well as other academic initiatives and faculty recruitment.

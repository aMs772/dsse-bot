## Biography

Raja Shaw is a Junior Administrative Assistant at the Desai Sethi School of Entrepreneurship (DSSE), IIT Bombay, where he is responsible for managing accounts and administrative tasks.

He holds a Master’s degree in Computer Applications from Lovely Professional University, following his Bachelor of Science from Calcutta University. Prior to joining DSSE, Raja gained over two years of experience as a Back Office Executive in the real estate sector, where he developed strong organizational and operational skills. His background in administration and technical education provides him with a well-rounded skill set to excel in his role.

## Biography

Sanjay Kharat is Assistant Project Manager at Desai Sethi School of Entrepreneurship, IIT Bombay. He holds a MBA in Human Resource from Jaipur National University after graduating with a Bachelor of Arts from Mumbai University. He joined IIT Bombay in 2011 and worked in the ‘Train 10,000 Teachers Training Program (T10KT)’, Low Cost Access Devices (LCAD) and NVLI. Before joining DSSE he worked for "National Centre of Excellence in Carbon Capture and Utilization" at IIT Bombay for 2 years.

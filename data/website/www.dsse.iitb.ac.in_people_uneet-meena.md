## Biography

Uneet Meena is a Project Manager at the Desai Sethi School of Entrepreneurship (DSSE), IIT Bombay, where he plays a key role in managing and supporting entrepreneurship programs.

He holds a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science from IIT Jodhpur, combining a strong academic foundation with a deep commitment to fostering innovation and driving entrepreneurial success. He works closely with students, faculty, and industry professionals to create a collaborative environment that nurtures practical learning and fosters entrepreneurial growth.
